# AYTHER

### Classic games. Reimagined.

**An open platform for reimagining the audiovisual presentation of classic games while preserving their original logic, gameplay and identity.**

[Website](https://ayther.dev) · [Explore AYTHER](https://github.com/Ayther-Dev/ayther) · [Roadmap](https://github.com/orgs/Ayther-Dev/projects)

## The past isn't replaced. It's reimagined.

Classic games are more than pixels, limited palettes and synthesized audio.

Their identity lives in their **gameplay, timing, mechanics and design**.

AYTHER preserves that foundation while allowing the audiovisual layer to be reimagined with modern assets — HD sprites, backgrounds, interfaces, audio and visual effects — **without rewriting the original game or modifying its ROM**.

Think of it as a new creative layer between a classic game and the screen.

> **The original game remains the game. AYTHER changes how it is presented.**
